## What you may publish

Publish lawful learning materials. Share copyrighted work only when you hold the necessary rights. Keep other people’s personal data out of titles, sources and lessons. Consider carefully before including your own personal information. Spam and advertising are prohibited.

## Moderation and reports

The platform moderates published material. The operator may remove content and restrict publishing. To report a problem, [open an issue](https://github.com/startodel/startodel/issues/new) with the public Track URL and a brief description. Keep personal or confidential information out of public reports.
